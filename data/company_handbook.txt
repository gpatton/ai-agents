AgentForge Employee Handbook

Annual Leave

Employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per year.
Employees must request annual leave at least 14 days in advance.
Unused annual leave may carry over to the following year,
up to a maximum of 5 days.

Remote Working

Employees may work remotely up to four days per week.
Employees must attend the Dublin office at least one day per week.

Learning and Development

Each employee receives an annual learning budget of €2,000.
The budget may be used for approved courses, certifications,
technical books and professional conferences.

Equipment

Remote employees receive a company laptop and a €500 allowance
for approved home-office equipment.
